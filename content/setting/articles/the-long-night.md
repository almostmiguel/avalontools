Stories disagree about how long the darkness lasted. They agree on the small lights that guided travelers from one shelter to the next.

## What remains

[[The Lantern Company]] preserves accounts carried along the roads. The lantern at [[The Crossroads]] is one of the places where the story is still told.

## Open questions

What caused the darkness, and what finally ended it? Different settlements remember different answers. The truth can remain a discovery for the table.
