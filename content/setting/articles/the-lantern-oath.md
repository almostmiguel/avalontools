> Keep a light. Share the road. Bring the story home.

The oath is a custom rather than a formal religion. It asks those with shelter to remember those without it, and those who travel to bring back news for the people they leave behind.

## Keeping the oath

A lantern in a window can mark a place of welcome. At [[The Crossroads]], [[Keeper Mara]] maintains a public flame in memory of [[The Long Night]].
