> Every place has a story. Every story leaves a mark.

This atlas brings together the places, people, and histories of Avalon. Follow the links between entries to explore the world through its connections.

## A place to begin

The sample settlement of [[The Crossroads]] connects the roads, travelers, and rumors of this starter atlas. Its lantern keeper, [[Keeper Mara]], offers a first face to put to the place.

## Threads of the world

[[The Lantern Company]] carries news along the roads. Its members keep [[The Lantern Oath]], a promise whose origins reach back to [[The Long Night]].
