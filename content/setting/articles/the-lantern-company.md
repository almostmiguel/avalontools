The company is held together by shared routes and a simple promise: carry news honestly, leave clear signs for those who follow, and never pass a traveler in need without stopping.

## Along the roads

Guides, couriers, and caravan guards recognize one another by a small lantern badge. At [[The Crossroads]], [[Keeper Mara]] maintains a place for members to exchange news.

## Origins

The company traces its customs to the travelers who helped one another during [[The Long Night]]. Their promises became [[The Lantern Oath]].
