Mara knows the sound of every regular caravan and remembers the names of people who visit only once. She keeps the lantern at [[The Crossroads]] lit and a spare chair beside the well.

## At the Crossroads

Travelers come to Mara for directions, introductions, and news. She rarely offers advice before hearing the whole story.

## The promise

Mara follows [[The Lantern Oath]]. She considers keeping a light for a stranger a small act with a long memory.
