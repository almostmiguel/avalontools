> All roads lead somewhere. Here, they stop long enough to tell you where.

Three roads meet around an old stone well and a lantern that is never allowed to go dark. Merchants arrive at dawn, guides exchange news at midday, and travelers gather around the light when the market closes.

## The market square

The square is the heart of the settlement. A message board beside the well carries requests for escorts, notices of missing caravans, and news from distant towns.

## The lantern

[[Keeper Mara]] tends the lantern. Its flame is a reminder of [[The Long Night]], and a welcome to anyone traveling after sunset.

## People and connections

Members of [[The Lantern Company]] use the square as a meeting place before setting out along the roads. Their presence keeps the settlement connected to the wider world of [[Avalon]].
